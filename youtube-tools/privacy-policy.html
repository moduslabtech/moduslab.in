Modus Lab YouTube tools: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Modus Lab Publisher and Modus Lab Coach ("the tools") are internal tools that Modus Lab uses to manage its own YouTube channel, @Modus_Lab. They are not offered to the public, and only the owner of the Modus Lab channel signs in to them. This policy explains what they access and why.

YouTube API Services

The tools use YouTube API Services. By using them you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tools access

DataWhy
The signed-in channel's videos: titles, descriptions, tags, privacy and schedule, thumbnails, captions and playlistsUploading Modus Lab's videos and keeping their details up to date
The channel's own statistics: views, watch time, impressions and click-through rate (YouTube Analytics and Reporting)Reviewing how Modus Lab's videos perform and deciding what to improve
Public information about other videos (search results, titles, view counts)Understanding what is trending in our subject area

The tools never read or change anyone else's channel, comments or private data.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the channel owner's own computer: the sign-in token, copies of our video details, and daily statistics. Nothing is sent to a Modus Lab server. Statistics and research notes are kept as long as they are useful for comparing results and are deleted when no longer needed.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and it is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions. Removing it stops the tools immediately; the locally stored copies can then be deleted from the computer.
