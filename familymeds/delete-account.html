Delete your FamilyMeds account

Delete it in the app

Open FamilyMeds, go to Settings → Delete my account and type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and the phone returns to the sign-in screen.

Or ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, email support@moduslab.in from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my FamilyMeds account". We delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What you can delete yourself in the app

  • Family members, with their medicines and dose history.
  • Medicines.
  • Clinic links: unlinking a clinic deletes every report you shared with it.

What is deleted with your account

  • Your login (email address and password hash).
  • If you created the family: the family, its members, medicines, dose history, clinic links and reports you shared from your phone. Everyone else in the family loses access.
  • If you joined someone else's family: your login and your access. Deleting in the app also unlinks the clinics on your phone, which deletes the reports you shared with those clinics. The family's records stay with the family's creator.

Deleting in the app takes effect immediately; by email, when we process your request. Our hosting provider's backups expire within 30 days. Prescriptions a clinic created for you stay in that clinic's MediStock records; ask the clinic to change or remove them.
